A party-pace, choose-your-own-adventure ride with a coloring twist benefiting Raleigh Community Kickstand!

$10 Suggested Donation

Where: Jaycee Park, 2405 Wade Ave

When: Saturday, August 8

Registration: 5:00 p.m.

Join us for our second annual alleycat and cookout! New to alleycats? Alleycats are fun, choose-your-own-adventure rides inspired by bike couriers. You’ll get a list of stops, plan a route, and hit as many stops as you can, or want to, before the cutoff.

Each stop earns you a random crayon. At the end, use your crayons to color in a drawing. The best ones win prizes!

Ride solo or party-pace with a group. This event is perfect for beginners, newcomers, veteran townies, and everyone in between.

After the ride, stick around for a cookout with hot dogs, including vegetarian and meat options, drinks, snacks, and a coloring contest. Free bicycle safety checks will also be available during registration.

Proceeds benefit Raleigh Community Kickstand, a collective providing free bicycle repairs, bikes, and training for people in need. See you there!

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an alleycat?

An alleycat is a type of bike ride modeled after bike couriers. At the start, you receive a manifest (list of stops). You then create a route, navigate to some or all the stops, and return before the cut-off time. Think choose-your-own-adventure scavenger hunt or a laid-back Amazing Race on bikes.

Do I need to be fast?

No! In fact, party-pace is strongly encouraged. The Melted Crayon has a coloring twist to make it less about being fast and more about having fun. Each stop earns you a crayon that you use to color in the flyer. Best drawings win prizes.

When is it?

Saturday, August 8th! Registration begins at 5pm and the alleycat kicks off promptly at 6pm. Ride for as little or as much as you want, but make it back before the cutoff at 8pm. Once you get back, the coloring contest will begin followed by grilling and chilling post ride!

Where is it?

The picnic shelter at Jaycee Park — 2405 Wade Avenue!

Do I need a team?

No, but alleycats are way more fun with a team. You don’t need to make one in advance, just show up, make new friends, and ride together! The bike scene is filled with cool and very approachable folks.

How far will I ride?

There is no fixed route! You decide which stops to visit and in what order. Ride to a couple of stops or try to reach them all. Depending on how many stops you visit and the route you choose, you could ride anywhere from about 8 to 20 miles.

Do I need to know the area?

Not necessarily! You will be given specific addresses to navigate to using whatever analog or digital means you’d like, so intimate knowledge of the area is not required.

How can I navigate safely?

The stops were chosen intentionally to be accessible via safer roads and greenways. We will have Raleigh greenway/cycle maps for folks to use. If you are of the digital persuasion, the Cyclers app is better at finding safe routes than Google cycle mode. For a full guide, check out the “Navigating the Spoke City” zine by Jared Harber.

How do I get to the event?

We encourage you to ride or take public transit to the event! In fact, you get one point already based on how you got to the event. To ride GoRaleigh/GoTriangle buses for free, you can download the Umo Mobility app and redeem the Oaks & Spokes benefit code: GO-4MN4-9SNU. Consider contributing to O&S by becoming a member if you aren’t already!

Can someone check my bike before I ride?

We will have a couple of mechanics with pumps and lube to help top off your bike and perform quick safety checks to make sure everything is in good working order. If you need some more in-depth repairs, come by community shop hours and we will help you get your bike sorted.

What happens if I get a mechanical issue while riding?

It happens sometimes! You can take a bus back to Jaycee Park, call a friend, or call one of us. To ride GoRaleigh/GoTriangle buses for free, you can download the Umo Mobility app and redeem the Oaks & Spokes benefit code: GO-4MN4-9SNU. Consider contributing to O&S by becoming a member if you aren’t already!

What do I need?

A bike, a helmet, a water bottle, lights, and a way to carry your manifest and a couple of small items.

You said there’s a cookout?

Yes! We will have hot dogs, veggie dogs, watermelon, snacks, and drinks. Everything a growing cyclist needs!

What about this coloring contest and prizes?

Prizes will go to the best drawings and some special rider categories. After those are selected, we raffle off the remaining prizes. We always try to get a ton of prizes, so almost everyone who participates can leave with something.

What if I can’t afford the suggested donation?

Then don’t worry about it! That’s why it’s suggested. No doggy turned away for lack of funds. But if you can donate $10-20, all proceeds after the cost of the cookout go to Raleigh Community Kickstand to support free bike repair.
